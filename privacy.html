MENON：夢　隱私權政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective date：2026-10-01
中文English刪除帳號 Delete account

一、簡單說

  • 不登入也能玩完整個遊戲。不登入時，我們不收集任何資料，進度只存在你的裝置上。
  • 只有在你自己選擇開啟「雲端存檔」並登入時，才會把登入用的 Email 和遊戲進度存到雲端，用來在不同裝置之間同步進度。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤、不賣資料、不和任何人分享資料做行銷。
  • 你隨時可以在遊戲裡刪除帳號，資料會一起刪除。

二、我們收集什麼

資料什麼時候用途
Email 地址用 Email 驗證碼、Google 或 Apple 登入雲端存檔時建立與辨識你的帳號、寄送登入驗證碼
帳號識別碼（一組隨機編號）登入時自動產生把雲端存檔對應到你的帳號
遊戲進度（收藏、星圖、成就、紀錄等）登入後，進度有變動時在你的裝置之間同步

用 Google 或 Apple 登入時，我們只取得 Email 與帳號識別碼；用 Apple 登入並選擇「隱藏我的電子郵件」時，我們只會收到 Apple 提供的轉寄地址。我們不取得你的通訊錄、位置、相片或其他帳號資料。

音量、文字大小、語言等設定只存在你的裝置上，不會上傳。

三、資料存放在哪裡、誰能看到

雲端存檔使用 Supabase（資料庫與登入服務）存放，傳輸全程加密（HTTPS）。資料庫設有權限規則，每個帳號只能讀寫自己的存檔。除了提供服務所需的 Supabase，以及你選擇的登入服務（Google、Apple）之外，我們不會把資料提供給任何第三方，除非法律要求。

四、保存多久、怎麼刪除

  • 資料會保存到你刪除帳號為止。
  • 在遊戲裡刪除：設定 → 雲端存檔 → 刪除帳號。帳號與雲端存檔會立即永久刪除。
  • 不在遊戲裡刪除：請見 刪除帳號說明，我們會在 30 天內處理。
  • 只想刪除雲端上的存檔、保留帳號：設定 → 雲端存檔 → 刪除雲端存檔。
  • 裝置上的進度：刪除 App（或清除瀏覽器網站資料）即可刪除。

五、兒童

本遊戲不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為兒童在未經同意下提供了資料，請聯絡我們，我們會刪除。

六、你的權利

你可以隨時查詢、更正或刪除你的資料，或停止使用雲端存檔（登出即可）。依你所在地的法律（例如個人資料保護法、GDPR），你可能還有其他權利，請聯絡我們行使。

七、政策變更

政策如有變更，會更新本頁的生效日期；重大變更會在遊戲內的更新說明中告知。

1. In short

  • You can play the whole game without signing in. Without signing in, we collect no data; your progress stays on your device.
  • Only if you choose to turn on Cloud Save and sign in do we store your sign-in email and game progress in the cloud, to sync your progress between devices.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling of data, no sharing for marketing.
  • You can delete your account in the game at any time, and your data is deleted with it.

2. What we collect

DataWhenPurpose
Email addressWhen you sign in to Cloud Save with an email code, Google or AppleCreate and identify your account; send sign-in codes
Account ID (a random identifier)Created automatically when you sign inLink your cloud save to your account
Game progress (collection, star map, achievements, records, etc.)After signing in, when your progress changesSync between your devices

With Google or Apple sign-in we receive only your email and account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's relay address. We do not access your contacts, location, photos or any other account data.

Settings such as volume, text size and language stay on your device and are never uploaded.

3. Where data is stored and who can see it

Cloud saves are stored with Supabase (database and authentication service). All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each account can only read and write its own save. Apart from Supabase, which provides the service, and the sign-in provider you choose (Google, Apple), we do not share your data with any third party unless required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete your account.
  • In the game: Settings → Cloud Save → Delete account. Your account and cloud save are permanently deleted immediately.
  • Outside the game: see Account deletion. We process requests within 30 days.
  • To delete only your cloud save and keep the account: Settings → Cloud Save → Delete cloud save.
  • Progress on your device: uninstall the app (or clear the site data in your browser).

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time, or stop using Cloud Save by signing out. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have additional rights; contact us to exercise them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page, and announce significant changes in the in-game update notes.
