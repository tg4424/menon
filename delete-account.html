MENON：夢　刪除帳號

Delete your MENON account · 隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

在遊戲裡刪除（最快）

  1. 打開 MENON，進入「設定」。
  2. 找到「雲端存檔」，確認已經登入要刪除的帳號。
  3. 按「刪除帳號」，再按一次確認。

登入帳號（Email、帳號識別碼）與雲端存檔會立即永久刪除，無法復原。這台裝置上的進度不會被刪除。

沒有辦法打開遊戲時

請在 GitHub Issues 留言「刪除帳號」，並寫下你登入用的 Email（也可以只寫 Email 的前幾個字，我們會再私下確認）。我們會在 30 天內刪除帳號與雲端存檔，並回覆你。

會刪除什麼

  • 刪除：Email、帳號識別碼、雲端存檔（收藏、星圖、成就、紀錄等）。
  • 不保留任何資料；雲端服務的系統備份可能在短時間內仍含有舊資料，之後會自動覆蓋。
  • 只想刪除雲端存檔、保留帳號：設定 → 雲端存檔 → 刪除雲端存檔。

Delete in the game (fastest)

  1. Open MENON and go to Settings.
  2. Under Cloud Save, make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap “Delete account”, then tap again to confirm.

Your sign-in (email and account ID) and your cloud save are permanently deleted immediately. This can't be undone. Progress stored on the device itself is not deleted.

If you can't open the game

Open an issue on GitHub Issues titled “Delete account” with the email you signed in with (or just its first few characters; we will confirm privately). We delete the account and cloud save within 30 days and reply to you.

What is deleted

  • Deleted: email, account ID and cloud save (collection, star map, achievements, records, etc.).
  • We keep no data. System backups at our cloud provider may briefly contain old data until they are overwritten automatically.
  • To delete only the cloud save and keep your account: Settings → Cloud Save → Delete cloud save.
