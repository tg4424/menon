MENON：夢　刪除帳號

Delete your MENON account · 隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

方法一：在遊戲裡刪除

Option 1: Delete in the game

  1. 打開 MENON，進入「設定」。Open MENON and go to Settings.
  2. 在「雲端存檔」確認已經登入要刪除的帳號。Under Cloud Save, make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  3. 按「刪除帳號」，再按一次確認。Tap “Delete account”, then tap again to confirm.

會刪除什麼

What is deleted

  • 立即永久刪除：Email、帳號識別碼、雲端存檔（收藏、星圖、成就、紀錄等）、每日挑戰排行紀錄。
    Deleted immediately and permanently: your email, account ID, cloud save (collection, star map, achievements, records, etc.) and daily challenge scores.
  • 不保留任何資料。雲端服務的系統備份可能短時間內還有舊資料，之後會自動覆蓋。
    We keep no data. Backups at our cloud provider may briefly contain old data until they are overwritten automatically.
  • 裝置上的進度不會被刪除；刪除 App（或清除瀏覽器的網站資料）即可刪除。
    Progress stored on your device is not deleted. Uninstall the app (or clear the site's data in your browser) to remove it.
  • 只想刪除雲端存檔、保留帳號：遊戲裡的 設定 → 雲端存檔 → 刪除雲端存檔。
    To delete only your cloud save and keep your account: in the game, Settings → Cloud Save → Delete cloud save.
  • 匿名遊戲數據不和帳號連結；可以在 設定 → 匿名遊戲數據 → 清除我的匿名數據 刪除。
    Anonymous gameplay data isn't linked to your account. Delete it in Settings → Anonymous game data → Delete my anonymous data.
